Lintel playground

github.com/Amsozzer1/lintel

Paste a Supabase migration and a few seed rows. Lintel applies them to Postgres, runs Supabase’s linter, then reads every table as the public anon key.

Runs entirely in this tab. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

Findings

The leaky table example runs as soon as Postgres has loaded.

Then pick another example or paste your own. Findings show up here the way the CLI prints them.

The CLI does more than this page can. It compares your branch against main, replays into Supabase’s real Postgres image instead of PGlite, and runs extensions like pg_cron for real. Here they are stubs that do nothing. On a repo: npx @amsozzer/lintel migrations check
